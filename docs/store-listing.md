## Full description (max 4000)
Want it now? Wait first.

WishWait helps you pause before you buy, so you only spend on things you'll still love next week. Ollie the owl is your friendly money buddy. He shows you what a wish really costs and keeps you company while you wait.

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
• See what a price really means for you, in hours of work
• A fair waiting time for every wish, big or small
• Gentle reminders and a quick "still want it?" check-in
• Extra care on big sale days and right after payday
• If you still want it, Ollie helps you pick a good day to buy

KEEP WHAT YOU DON'T SPEND
• Every wish you let go adds to the money you kept
• Save toward one simple goal
• Earn badges for patience and smart choices

EASY TO USE
• Share a product link from your shopping app to add it fast
• A home screen widget for your next countdown
• Light and dark mode

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads
• Your wishes stay on your phone
• Optional backup to your own Google Drive

WishWait is free. If Ollie helped you save, you can leave an optional thank-you tip. Nothing is ever locked.

WishWait gives estimates to help you think. It isn't financial advice, and every decision is yours.
